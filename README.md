## 🤖 What Is Aurum-Clipper?

Aurum-Clipper is a smart video tool that automatically finds the most interesting parts of any video and turns them into ready-to-post short clips. Whether you have a long YouTube video, a podcast, or any other recording, Aurum-Clipper uses artificial intelligence to identify the best moments, add cool animated subtitles, and prepare everything for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts.

## 🎯 Who Is This For?

- Marketers who need quick promotional clips
- Anyone who wants to repurpose long videos into engaging short content

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI-Powered Virality Scoring
Aurum-Clipper uses Google Gemini to analyze every part of your video. It ranks each moment by how likely it is to go viral, so you always get the most engaging segments.

### 🎤 Automatic Transcription
The tool listens to your video and writes down everything that's said using local Whisper transcription. This happens on your computer—no need to upload your video anywhere.

### 💬 Kinetic Subtitles
Get beautiful animated subtitles (ASS format) that move and highlight words as they're spoken. These captions grab viewers' attention and make your clips more accessible.

### ✂️ Smart Trimming
Aurum-Clipper automatically detects natural breaks in conversation, removes silences, and cuts clips at the perfect moment so they feel polished and professional.

### 📱 Social Media Ready
Every clip is formatted for the exact dimensions and duration that work best on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### The app won't open
Make sure you have the latest version of Windows installed. Try right-clicking the app and selecting "Run as administrator."

### Processing is very slow
Close other programs to free up memory. If you have an older computer, try processing shorter videos.

### No clips are found
Make sure your video has clear audio. Videos with no speech or very quiet audio might not get good results.

### Subtitles look wrong
Check that your video language is set correctly in settings. Aurum-Clipper auto-detects but sometimes you need to select manually.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- Use videos with clear, loud audio for better transcription
